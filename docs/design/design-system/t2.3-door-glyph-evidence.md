## Measured contract delta

| Consumer | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Raised shared door row | 17pt `.headline`, medium | `IconRole.rowRaised`: 20pt relative to `.headline` through `TypographyRole.listRowTitle` |
| Hairline shared door row | 17pt `.headline`, medium | `IconRole.rowQuiet`: 18pt relative to `.subheadline` through `TypographyRole.button` |
| Explore quiet destination | 18pt relative to `.body`, medium | `IconRole.rowQuiet`: 18pt relative to `.subheadline`, with a local `.fontWeight(.medium)` override |
| Explore quiet destination icon column | Fixed 24pt | 24pt at the default content size, scaled relative to `.subheadline` at larger content sizes |

The Explore override deliberately preserves the shipped no-pulse behavior. A hostile emphasized
control environment renders differently if that override is removed; the point-of-use test covers
both default and AX and was proven to fail under that mutation.

`ExploreSurfaceIconGeometry.scopeControl` remains 20, and `ExploreScopeControlGlyph` remains
body-anchored. Its numeric equality with `rowRaised` is not semantic equivalence: Scope/site 2
belongs to T2.8 and is outside this diff.

## AX5 divergence repair

The first post-migration AX5 capture exposed a collision: the `rowQuiet` symbol scaled, but the
fixed 24pt icon column did not, so the glyph escaped into the subtitle. A8's frozen AX frame is
clean, which made this an implementation divergence inside the owned quiet row rather than a
reason to restructure the sheet. `ExploreQuietDestinationIconColumn` now scales that 24pt base
relative to `.subheadline`; the scrollable root, row order, copy, and chevron geometry remain
unchanged. The repaired AX5 UI test also requires Settings and About to become fully contained in
the app frame, proving both horizontal separation and vertical reachability.

## Visual inspection

- Default before and after: Settings and About remain fully visible; their labels, glyphs, and
  chevrons are not clipped.
- AX5 dark before: the exact-base capture records the gear and book colliding with destination
  metadata, with About only partly visible at the lower fold.
- AX5 dark after: the evidence is intentionally scroll-advanced so Settings and About are fully
  contained. Both scaled glyphs have clear separation from their subtitles and chevrons, and the
  complete About row and lower divider remain reachable without clipping.
- Navigation, copy, row-title typography, accessibility identifiers, and button morphology are
  unchanged.
